Presentation Speaking Notes

Speaker: Malitha Tishamal (Lead Developer)
"Good morning everyone. Welcome to the presentation of the Eduwide App. I am Malitha Tishamal, the Lead Developer, and today our team of 7 will walk you through our Educational Management System.

I will start by explaining our technology stack. We built this app using Flutter to provide a seamless cross-platform experience. For our backend, we utilized Firebase. As the lead, I designed the core database architecture using Firestore—creating structured NoSQL collections like 'users', 'enrollments', 'zoomMeetings', and 'payments' to efficiently handle our complex relational data and ensure real-time synchronization across all devices." (Action: Switch slide to Login/Signup Screens)
Speaker: Team Member 2
"Thank you, Malitha. Moving to the first step of the user journey, I developed the Authentication User Interfaces. I built the Login and Sign-Up screens, ensuring we capture essential data like Student ID, A/L Year, and Stream. I also implemented the form validations and the 'Forgot Password' UI to provide a smooth onboarding experience."
Speaker: Malitha Tishamal (Lead Developer)
"Once the user hits 'Login', our backend security takes over. I engineered the Role-Based Routing system through a core component called the DashboardWrapper. It securely verifies the user via Firebase Authentication, reads their assigned role in real-time from our database, and dynamically routes them to either the Admin, Teacher, or Student dashboard, preventing any unauthorized access." (Action: Switch slide to Admin Dashboard)
Speaker: Team Member 3
"As Malitha explained, new users are securely routed. However, new accounts are set as 'pending'. I built the Admin Dashboard and the User Accounts Management screen. This allows administrators to view pending accounts, verify student details, and officially approve or lock users before they can access the platform."
Speaker: Team Member 4
"After approval, students need to be assigned to classes. I developed the Class Enrollment interface. This allows Admins to seamlessly add students to specific teachers' classes. I also built the Global Resource Upload section, giving admins the power to distribute study materials across the app."
Speaker: Team Member 5
"On the educator's side, I designed the Teacher Dashboard. When a teacher logs in, they can instantly see statistics and live lists of their newly enrolled students, categorized by academic year. I also implemented the Teacher Profile management screens." (Action: Switch slide to Zoom & Payments)
Speaker: Malitha Tishamal (Lead Developer)
"This brings us to the most advanced functionality of our app: Live Classes and Payments. I engineered the backend logic to integrate Zoom Classes directly into our platform. Teachers can schedule sessions, and the app instantly syncs these active meetings to Firestore so students can join.

Furthermore, I built the Payment Gateway Architecture. When a student is enrolled, my database logic automatically calculates and tracks their payments. This provides administrators and teachers with a real-time summary of 'Pending Payments' versus 'Completed Payments', giving our platform complete financial oversight."
Speaker: Team Member 6
"Finally, the student logs in. I developed the Student Dashboard. Here, students can view their enrolled subjects, check their payment status, download uploaded resources, and click a single button to seamlessly launch into the live Zoom sessions created by their teachers."
Speaker: Team Member 7
"To tie this entire flow together, I managed the Global UI and UX. I integrated our custom Blue Theme across all screens to ensure a consistent, modern aesthetic. I also conducted cross-platform QA testing to guarantee the app runs smoothly on Android, iOS, and Web."
Speaker: Malitha Tishamal (Lead Developer)
"In conclusion, by successfully dividing our frontend tasks among the team while centralizing our complex database architecture, secure routing, and advanced integrations, we were able to build a highly secure, scalable, and modern educational platform. Thank you for listening. We are now open for any questions."
